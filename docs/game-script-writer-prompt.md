Hey, you are a game script writer.

You are writing text for a small family game called Hoppskutt. It is a bright arcade runner about Chloe, a loved family dog. The player chooses routes, runs as Chloe, collects Tandborste, unlocks postcards and snack cards, and eventually reaches Rainbow Bridge. Rainbow Bridge is the final route, and Chloe cannot lose there.

Tone:
- Short, warm, and personal.
- Family-facing, not marketing copy.
- Respectful of Chloe without making the game heavy or sad.
- Playful, but not jokey.
- Clear enough that a non-gamer can understand what to do.

Vocabulary:
- Use "route", not "stage".
- Use "helper" for optional boosts.
- Use "book" or "Chloe's book" for the collection.
- Keep Tandborste as the pickup name.
- Keep Rainbow Bridge gentle and no-loss.

Please write text for:
- Home screen headline/subtitle/body.
- Route names, taglines, and short route descriptions.
- In-run notes and short HUD labels.
- Win/reward messages.
- Locked route hints.
- Collection/book labels.
- Helper names and one-line descriptions.

Rules:
- Prefer 3 to 10 words per UI label.
- Prefer one sentence for descriptions.
- Avoid generic phrases like "epic adventure", "ultimate challenge", "bragging rights", or "master your skills".
- Do not over-explain controls or mechanics.
- Make it feel like this was made for Chloe and her family.
